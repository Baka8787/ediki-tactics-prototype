# GYM. gym-opening with ONE number changed: guardCost 3 -> 5 on the party.
#
# Map, enemies, spawn positions, AI and every other player stat are byte-
# identical to gym-opening.encounter. Only the guard price differs.
#
# THE HYPOTHESIS (專案負責人, after playing): base stats plus rotating who
# tanks is enough to win, skills are optional, and Guard may be too strong.
#
# The arithmetic says the last part is the root:
#   move 1 (1) + attack (4) + guard (3) = 8 AP = exactly one turn's regen,
#   so guard is free. And guard is worth 100-140 HP a turn against two
#   attackers, against 40 damage for the attack it would have displaced.
#
# At guardCost 5 the two stop fitting together (4 + 5 = 9 > 8), but the AP cap
# is 10 — so banking 2 AP buys attack + guard next turn. It converts a free
# action into one you pay a turn of tempo for.
encounter id=gym-opening-guard5 name=OPENING-guard5
objective type=rout turns=20
