# GYM. gym-opening with ONE number changed: the clock, 20 -> 30 rounds.
#
# WHY IT EXISTS. Every defensive strategy on gym-opening loses while ending the
# battle with roughly half the party's HP still on the bar:
#
#   corridor-hold  29% win   19.31 rounds   550 HP left (of 1150)
#   shield-wall     6% win   20.42 rounds   463 HP left
#   charge         56% win   10.68 rounds   142 HP left
#
# Units that end with 550 HP are not being killed. They are running out of
# turns. So this file asks the one question that separates the two readings:
# are the defensive lines LOSING FIGHTS, or losing races?
#
# If the win rates converge at 30 rounds, the 20-round clock was the whole
# story and it over-corrected — it was added to stop scripted strategies
# stalling forever against immobile turrets, and a clock punishes that symptom
# by flattening every decision that costs tempo. If they do not converge, the
# defensive lines really are worse and the clock was innocent.
encounter id=gym-opening-clock30 name=OPENING-clock30
objective type=rout turns=30
