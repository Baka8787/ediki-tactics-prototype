# GYM. gym-opening with ONE variable changed: ENEMY TARGET SELECTION.
#
# Every enemy is ai=hunter (targets the LOWEST HP) instead of rusher/cautious
# (target the NEAREST). Roster, map, spawn cells and objective are untouched.
#
# The other half of the §18.4 experiment. gym-opening-ranged4 attacks damage
# routing with REACH (shoot past the blocker); this attacks it with INTENT
# (walk around the blocker and go for the fragile one).
#
# The two are deliberately separate files. Ranged-ness and target preference
# are different rules and changing both at once would tell us nothing about
# either — the mistake the boss ladder nearly made in §16.
#
# ⚠️ Expected to be the WEAKER of the two: units block movement (OD-03), so a
# hunter that wants the back row still has to find a way round. On an open
# field it can; in a corridor it cannot. That limit is the point of measuring.
encounter id=gym-opening-hunter name=OPENING-hunter
objective type=rout turns=20
