# WHAT IT IS FOR. M6 can only ever FALSIFY: a fixed strategy has a fixed
# preference, so the same map gives corridor-hold 26/43/29 and charge 3/96/0
# (playtest-metrics §9.5). A route histogram on its own therefore cannot say
# how much of the distribution came from the MAP. This map is the missing
# comparison — three gaps in the same places, same block, same spawns, same
# enemies, and nothing to tell the routes apart:
#
#   west x=2    mid x=6    east x=10    all 1 wide, all road, all exposure 2
#
# F3 reads it: if gym-d1-routes' M6 differs from this by less than 10 points,
# the distribution is strategy preference and the geometry bought nothing.
# It is also the comparison OD-26 has been missing since it was opened.
#
# THE ONE VARIABLE. Against gym-d1-routes exactly three things changed, and
# they are the three the plan names as one bundle ("同寬、同地形、同暴露"):
#   - the mid lane narrows from 3 cells to 1
#   - the two forest cells in the east lane become road
#   - the west lane is untouched
#
# Sealing each lane and measuring the path gives 9 / 11 / 9 AP here against
# 9 / 10 / 10 on the experiment map. The mid lane costing one more is a
# property of a block sitting under it, and widening mid is what paid that
# cost back on the experiment map — a CONSEQUENCE of the variable, not a
# second one. Stated here rather than left for someone to find in the numbers.
#
