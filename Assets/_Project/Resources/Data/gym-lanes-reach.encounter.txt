# GYM. gym-lanes with ONE thing changed: the objective.
#
# Why this variant exists. Under OD-16 every enemy closes in from turn 1, so
# with a rout objective the fight walks to the player and the player never has
# to travel. M6 measured exactly that: on gym-lanes/rout, 133 of 200 battles
# never touched the wall row. Three well-built routes cannot be a decision in a
# battle where nobody goes anywhere.
#
# So this file holds the map and the spawns FIXED and changes only the
# objective, to separate "the routes are bad" from "nothing made the player
# use them".
#
# The target sits at the top of the MID lane on purpose: mid is the shortest
# way there and also the most exposed (a 9x6 open field, exposure 4), while
# west is a 1-wide corridor and east is forest. Fast-and-dangerous against
# slow-and-safe is the trade the routes are supposed to offer.
#
# NOT a decision about what Stage 01 becomes. Objective choice is OD-19.

encounter id=gym-lanes-reach name=LANES-reach
objective type=reach x=12 y=1 turns=10
